1. ECG

ECG (Electrocardiogram) is a graphical recording of the electrical activity of the heart.

  • Measures electrical activity of heart
  • Helps diagnose arrhythmias
  • Detects heart abnormalities

2. PQRST Complex

  • P Wave: Atrial depolarization
  • QRS Complex: Ventricular depolarization
  • T Wave: Ventricular repolarization

3. Five Steps of Rhythm Analysis

  1. Determined regularity
    • Equal R-R Intervals -> Regular Rhythm
    • Unequal R-R Intervals -> Irregular Rhythm


  2. Calculate Heart Rate
    • 300 Method: 300 divided by number of big boxes

      • => Use the diagram sequence between two normal and regular QRS complex to find the heart rate.

    • 1500 Method: 1500 divided by number of small boxes

      • => Count number of small boxes between two QRS complex in a strip, then divide by 1500.

    • 6 Second Method: Count QRS in 6 seconds × 10

      • => Count no. of heart wave in a 6 second strip, then multiply by 10.



  3. Access P Waves

    4. (0.12 - 0.20 seconds)

    1. Present or Absent P Waves
    2. One P wave before each QRS complex
    3. Same shape and size


  4. Measure PR Interval

    5. (0.12 - 0.20 seconds)

    1. If the P wave duration is less than 0.12 seconds, then the rhythm is called Short P-R Interval
    2. If the P wave duration is greater than 0.20 seconds, then the rhythm is called First Degree AV Block


  5. Evaluate QRS Complex

    6. (0.04 - 0.10 seconds)

    1. If the rhythm is greater than 0.10 seconds, then the rhythm is called IVCD[IntraVentricular Conduction Delay] Bundle Branch Block[BBB]
    2. Because, AV node supply the impulse with delay, QRS complex - wide.
