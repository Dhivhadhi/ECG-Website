1. Atrial Fibrillation

  • Irregularly irregular rhythm
  • No identical P waves
  • Above 350 BPM
  • P waves are chotic, wavy, vibrating or erotic

2. Atrial Flutter

  • Sawtooth P waves called flutter wave or F wave
  • Regular or Irregular rhythm
  • 250 - 350 BPM
  • No P waves, instead flutter waves are present

Atrial Fibrillation VS Atrial Flutter

3. Supraventricular Tachycardia (SVT)

  • 180 - 250 BPM
  • Very or Highly regular rhythm.
  • P wave burried in T wave.
  • Narrow QRS complex
  • It is also called Regularly Regular Rhythm or Narrow QRS complex Tachycardia

4. Wandering Atrial Pacemaker (WAP)

  • Irregular rhythm
  • Variable P wave morphology
  • Heart rate below 100 BPM
  • Minimum 3 different P wave morphologies changes

5. Multi-focal Atrial Tachycardia (MAT)

  • Irregular rhythm
  • Variable P wave morphology
  • Heart rate above 100 BPM
  • Minimum 3 different P wave morphologies changes
