PREMATURE JUNCTIONAL CONTRACTIONS (PJC)

  • It occurs before the expected time.
  • Compensatory pause follows the premature beat.
  • There should be a time gap between the premature beat and the next normal beat.
  • P wave is absent or inverted or retrograde.
  • QRS complex is normal.
