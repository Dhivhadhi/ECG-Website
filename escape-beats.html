TYPES OF ESCAPE BEATS

  1. Atrial Escape Beats
  2. Junctional Escape Beats
  3. Ventricular Escape Beats

1. Atrial Escape Beats

  • It occurs the expected time.
  • No compensatory pause after the escape beat.
  • P wave morphology is changing from normal sinus beat.
  • QRS complex is normal.

2. Junctional Escape Beats

  • It occurs the expected time.
  • No compensatory pause after the escape beat.
  • P wave is absent or inverted or retrograde.
  • QRS complex is normal.

3. Ventricular Escape Beats

  • It occurs the expected time.
  • No compensatory pause after the escape beat.
  • P wave is absent.
  • QRS complex is wide.
