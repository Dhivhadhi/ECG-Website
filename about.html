About This Website

This ECG educational website is designed for students, nurses, medical professionals, and beginners who want to learn ECG interpretation.


The website includes ECG basics, rhythm analysis, arrhythmias, heart blocks, escape beats, lethal rhythms, diagrams, and tables.


Our goal is to make ECG learning simple, visual, and understandable.
